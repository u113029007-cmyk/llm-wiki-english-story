# spring cleaning

## 意思
- n. 大掃除（春季大掃除）。中文解釋是翻譯，句意依據原文例句。[src: mrs-tittlemouse ¶67]

## 原文例句
- "began a spring cleaning which lasted a fortnight." [src: mrs-tittlemouse ¶67]
- "She had been having a complete turn out and spring-cleaning, to relieve her feelings." [src: mr-tod ¶149]
