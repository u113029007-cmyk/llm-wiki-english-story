# cousin

## 意思
- n. 表（堂）兄弟姊妹。中文解釋是翻譯，句意依據原文例句。[src: benjamin-bunny ¶9]

## 原文例句
- "nearly tumbled upon the top of his Cousin Peter." [src: benjamin-bunny ¶9]
- "he had heard about cats from his cousin, little Benjamin Bunny." [src: peter-rabbit ¶27]
- "he married his Cousin Flopsy." [src: flopsy-bunnies ¶5]
- "Cousin Peter! Peter Rabbit, Peter Rabbit!" [src: mr-tod ¶27]
