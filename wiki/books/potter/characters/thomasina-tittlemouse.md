# Thomasina Tittlemouse／Mrs. Tittlemouse（湯瑪西娜・提多老鼠）

## 基本資料
- 一隻長尾巴的林鼠："she was called Thomasina Tittlemouse, a wood mouse with a long tail." [src: flopsy-bunnies ¶14]
- 她說她認識 Peter Rabbit。[src: flopsy-bunnies ¶16]

## 在 mrs-tittlemouse 篇
- （推論）第五篇的主角 Mrs. Tittlemouse 和本頁是同一隻：兩篇都說她是林鼠，第五篇也出現全名 Mrs. Thomasina Tittlemouse。[src: flopsy-bunnies ¶14] [src: mrs-tittlemouse ¶2] [src: mrs-tittlemouse ¶53]
- 住在樹籬下的土坡裡，非常愛乾淨。[src: mrs-tittlemouse ¶3] [src: mrs-tittlemouse ¶7]
- 不喜歡不請自來的訪客，把甲蟲、瓢蟲、蜘蛛都趕出去。[src: mrs-tittlemouse ¶9-15]
- 被 Mr. Jackson 弄亂家後，大掃除兩週，開派對時沒請他。[src: mrs-tittlemouse ¶61] [src: mrs-tittlemouse ¶67] [src: mrs-tittlemouse ¶69]

## 在 flopsy-bunnies 篇做了什麼
- 在垃圾堆翻找時吵醒 Benjamin，一直道歉。[src: flopsy-bunnies ¶15-16]
- 躲在果醬罐裡。[src: flopsy-bunnies ¶17]
- 很足智多謀，在麻袋角咬洞救出小兔子："But Mrs. Tittlemouse was a resourceful person." [src: flopsy-bunnies ¶30]
- 下一個聖誕節收到兔毛禮物。[src: flopsy-bunnies ¶57]
- （推論）這份禮物可能是救小兔子的謝禮；原文沒說是誰送、為什麼送。[src: flopsy-bunnies ¶30-31] [src: flopsy-bunnies ¶57]
