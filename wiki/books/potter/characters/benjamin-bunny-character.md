# Benjamin Bunny（班傑明兔）

## 基本資料
- Peter 的表弟，Peter 從他那裡聽說過貓的事："he had heard about cats from his cousin, little Benjamin Bunny." [src: peter-rabbit ¶27]
- 在 peter-rabbit 篇沒有出場，只被提到一次。[src: peter-rabbit ¶27]
- 爸爸是 old Mr. Benjamin Bunny。[src: benjamin-bunny ¶28]

## 個性與特徵
- 蹦蹦跳跳出門："with a hop, skip, and a jump" [src: benjamin-bunny ¶5]
- 不太想見阿姨。[src: benjamin-bunny ¶8]
- 穿木鞋。[src: benjamin-bunny ¶21]
- 在花園裡很自在，常和爸爸來拿萵苣："Benjamin, on the contrary, was perfectly at home" [src: benjamin-bunny ¶27]
- 遇到貓時反應很快，立刻把大家藏進籃子。[src: benjamin-bunny ¶37]

## 做了什麼
- 帶 Peter 從梨樹爬進花園拿回衣服，還裝洋蔥送阿姨。[src: benjamin-bunny ¶18] [src: benjamin-bunny ¶22-25]
- 被爸爸拎著耳朵拉出籃子並挨打。[src: benjamin-bunny ¶52]

## 長大以後
- 娶了表妹 Flopsy，有很多孩子。[src: flopsy-bunnies ¶5]
- 食物不夠時向 Peter 借高麗菜。[src: flopsy-bunnies ¶7]
- 在垃圾堆睡前把紙袋套在頭上擋蒼蠅。[src: flopsy-bunnies ¶12]
- 孩子被 Tommy Brock 偷走，立刻追出去，找 Peter 幫忙。[src: mr-tod ¶21] [src: mr-tod ¶27]
- 這次換他驚慌、猶豫，被 Peter 催著進屋救孩子。[src: mr-tod ¶139] [src: mr-tod ¶144] [src: mr-tod ¶150]
