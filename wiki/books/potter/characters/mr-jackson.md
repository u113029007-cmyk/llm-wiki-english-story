# Mr. Jackson（傑克森先生）

## 基本資料
- 住在樹籬下排水溝、又髒又濕的水溝裡。[src: mrs-tittlemouse ¶32]
- 熊蜂罵他 "nasty old toad"（討厭的老蟾蜍）。[src: mrs-tittlemouse ¶57]
- （推論）是蟾蜍：敘述沒有直接說，根據是熊蜂的罵法，以及他住在濕水溝、全身滴水。[src: mrs-tittlemouse ¶32] [src: mrs-tittlemouse ¶35] [src: mrs-tittlemouse ¶57]

## 個性與特徵
- 一直微笑，坐滿整張小搖椅。[src: mrs-tittlemouse ¶31]
- 一顆牙都沒有："he certainly had not a tooth in his head." [src: mrs-tittlemouse ¶38]
- 口頭禪："Tiddly, widdly, widdly" [src: mrs-tittlemouse ¶42]
- 從不擦腳，到處留下濕腳印。[src: mrs-tittlemouse ¶28] [src: mrs-tittlemouse ¶44]
- 不怕被螫："He seemed to have no objection to stings." [src: mrs-tittlemouse ¶59]
- 沒被邀請也不生氣："he was not at all offended." [src: mrs-tittlemouse ¶71]

## 做了什麼
- 不請自來，為了找蜂蜜翻遍她家，最後把蜂巢拉出來。[src: mrs-tittlemouse ¶42-45] [src: mrs-tittlemouse ¶59]
