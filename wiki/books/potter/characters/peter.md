# Peter（彼得兔）

## 基本資料
- 四隻小兔子之一，兄弟姊妹是 Flopsy、Mopsy、Cottontail。[src: peter-rabbit ¶1]
- 和媽媽住在大冷杉樹根下。[src: peter-rabbit ¶2]
- 表弟是 little Benjamin Bunny："he had heard about cats from his cousin, little Benjamin Bunny." [src: peter-rabbit ¶27]

## 個性與特徵
- 很調皮："But Peter, who was very naughty" [src: peter-rabbit ¶8]
- 穿一件全新的藍外套，有黃銅鈕扣："It was a blue jacket with brass buttons, quite new." [src: peter-rabbit ¶16]
- 常弄丟衣服：兩週內弄丟第二套外套和鞋子。[src: peter-rabbit ¶33]
- 被追時非常害怕："Peter was most dreadfully frightened" [src: peter-rabbit ¶14]
- 第二篇開頭身體不舒服，只裹著紅色棉手帕。[src: benjamin-bunny ¶10]
- 再進花園時一直聽到聲音、不開心，想回家。[src: benjamin-bunny ¶26] [src: benjamin-bunny ¶30]
- 害怕時眼睛睜得像棒棒糖一樣大。[src: benjamin-bunny ¶34]
- 回家後媽媽因為衣服找回來而原諒他。[src: benjamin-bunny ¶58]

## 長大以後
- 開了一個苗圃，Benjamin 會向他借高麗菜："Flopsy's brother, Peter Rabbit, who kept a nursery garden." [src: flopsy-bunnies ¶7]
- 老鼠 Thomasina Tittlemouse 說她認識 Peter。[src: flopsy-bunnies ¶16]
- 還是穿藍外套，在草地找蒲公英。[src: mr-tod ¶27]
- 變得冷靜、會推理，推斷出 Tommy Brock 的去向，並帶 Benjamin 救孩子。[src: mr-tod ¶35-36] [src: mr-tod ¶138]
