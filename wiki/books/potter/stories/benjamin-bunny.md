# The Tale of Benjamin Bunny（班傑明兔的故事）

- 所屬：[[potter-overview]]
- 前一篇：[[peter-rabbit]]
- 下一篇：[[flopsy-bunnies]]

## 故事摘要（依段落順序）
### 1. Benjamin 出門找親戚
- 獻詞寫給 Sawrey 的孩子們。[src: benjamin-bunny ¶1]
  - 原文："For the children of Sawrey from Old Mr. Bunny" [src: benjamin-bunny ¶1]
- 一天早上，一隻小兔子坐在土坡上，聽見小馬的蹄聲。[src: benjamin-bunny ¶2-3]
  - 原文："One morning a little rabbit sat on a bank." [src: benjamin-bunny ¶2]
- Mr. McGregor 駕著雙輪馬車經過，Mrs. McGregor 戴著她最好的帽子坐在旁邊。[src: benjamin-bunny ¶4]
  - 原文："A gig was coming along the road; it was driven by Mr. McGregor, and beside him sat Mrs. McGregor in her best bonnet." [src: benjamin-bunny ¶4]
- 馬車過去後，Benjamin 溜到路上，蹦蹦跳跳去拜訪住在花園後面樹林裡的親戚。[src: benjamin-bunny ¶5]
  - 原文："to call upon his relations, who lived in the wood at the back of Mr. McGregor's garden." [src: benjamin-bunny ¶5]
- 親戚就是他的阿姨/姑姑（原文 aunt）和表兄弟姊妹（原文 cousins） Flopsy、Mopsy、Cottontail、Peter，住在樹林裡最整齊、沙最多的兔子洞。[src: benjamin-bunny ¶6]
  - 原文："in the neatest, sandiest hole of all lived Benjamin's aunt and his cousins" [src: benjamin-bunny ¶6]
- 兔媽媽是寡婦，靠織兔毛手套和腕套、賣香草、迷迭香茶和兔子菸草（也就是薰衣草）維生。[src: benjamin-bunny ¶7]
  - 原文："she earned her living by knitting rabbit-wool mittens and muffatees" [src: benjamin-bunny ¶7]

### 2. 找到 Peter
- Benjamin 不太想見阿姨/姑姑，繞到冷杉樹後面，差點跌到 Peter 身上。[src: benjamin-bunny ¶8-9]
  - 原文："Little Benjamin did not very much want to see his Aunt." [src: benjamin-bunny ¶8]
- Peter 獨自坐著，看起來不舒服，身上只裹著一條紅色棉手帕："He looked poorly, and was dressed in a red cotton pocket-handkerchief." [src: benjamin-bunny ¶10]
- Benjamin 小聲問誰拿了他的衣服，Peter 說："The scarecrow in Mr. McGregor's garden" [src: benjamin-bunny ¶11-12]
- Benjamin 說 McGregor 夫婦整天都不在，因為 Mrs. McGregor 戴了最好的帽子。[src: benjamin-bunny ¶13]
  - 原文："certainly for the day, because she was wearing her best bonnet." [src: benjamin-bunny ¶13]
- 兔媽媽在洞裡喊 Cottontail 再去拿些洋甘菊。[src: benjamin-bunny ¶15]
  - 原文："Cottontail! Cottontail! fetch some more camomile!" [src: benjamin-bunny ¶15]
- Peter 說去散步也許會舒服一點。[src: benjamin-bunny ¶16]
  - 原文："Peter said he thought he might feel better if he went for a walk." [src: benjamin-bunny ¶16]

### 3. 從梨樹爬進花園
- 兩人手牽手走到樹林底的牆頂，往下就看到花園，稻草人身上掛著 Peter 的外套和鞋子，還戴著 Mr. McGregor 的舊蘇格蘭帽。[src: benjamin-bunny ¶17]
  - 原文："Peter's coat and shoes were plainly to be seen upon the scarecrow" [src: benjamin-bunny ¶17]
  - 原文："They went away hand in hand, and got upon the flat top of the wall at the bottom of the wood." [src: benjamin-bunny ¶17]
- Benjamin 說鑽大門會弄壞衣服，正確的進法是爬下梨樹："the proper way to get in is to climb down a pear-tree." [src: benjamin-bunny ¶18]
- Peter 頭朝下摔下去，但下面是剛耙過的軟苗床，種著萵苣。[src: benjamin-bunny ¶19-20]
  - 原文："Peter fell down head first; but it was of no consequence, as the bed below was newly raked and quite soft." [src: benjamin-bunny ¶19]
- 他們在苗床上留下很多小腳印，特別是穿木鞋的 Benjamin。[src: benjamin-bunny ¶21]
  - 原文："They left a great many odd little footmarks all over the bed, especially little Benjamin, who was wearing clogs." [src: benjamin-bunny ¶21]

### 4. 拿回衣服、裝洋蔥
- 從稻草人身上拿回衣服；昨晚下過雨，鞋子裡有水，外套縮水了。[src: benjamin-bunny ¶23]
  - 原文："there was water in the shoes, and the coat was somewhat shrunk." [src: benjamin-bunny ¶23]
- Benjamin 試戴那頂帽子，太大了。[src: benjamin-bunny ¶24]
  - 原文："Benjamin tried on the tam-o'-shanter, but it was too big for him." [src: benjamin-bunny ¶24]
- Benjamin 提議用手帕裝洋蔥，當作送阿姨/姑姑的禮物。[src: benjamin-bunny ¶25]
  - 原文："as a little present for his Aunt." [src: benjamin-bunny ¶25]
- Peter 一直聽到聲音，玩得不開心；Benjamin 卻很自在，還吃萵苣葉，說他常和爸爸來這裡拿萵苣當週日晚餐。[src: benjamin-bunny ¶26-27]
  - 原文："Peter did not seem to be enjoying himself; he kept hearing noises." [src: benjamin-bunny ¶26]
  - 原文："He said that he was in the habit of coming to the garden with his father to get lettuces for their Sunday dinner." [src: benjamin-bunny ¶27]
- Peter 什麼都沒吃，想回家，還掉了一半洋蔥。[src: benjamin-bunny ¶30]
  - 原文："Peter did not eat anything; he said he should like to go home." [src: benjamin-bunny ¶30]

### 5. 遇到貓、躲進籃子
- 帶著蔬菜爬不回梨樹，Benjamin 帶路往花園另一頭走，沿著陽光照著的紅磚牆下的木板小路。[src: benjamin-bunny ¶31]
  - 原文："They went along a little walk on planks, under a sunny, red brick wall." [src: benjamin-bunny ¶31]
  - 原文："Little Benjamin said that it was not possible to get back up the pear-tree with a load of vegetables." [src: benjamin-bunny ¶31]
- Peter 聽到的聲音越來越可怕，眼睛睜得像棒棒糖一樣大："his eyes were as big as lolly-pops!" [src: benjamin-bunny ¶34]
- 轉角處看到了某個東西（原文由插圖呈現，文字沒寫），Benjamin 立刻把自己、Peter 和洋蔥藏到一個大籃子底下。[src: benjamin-bunny ¶36-37]
  - 原文："This is what those little rabbits saw round that corner!" [src: benjamin-bunny ¶36]
- 文字第一次寫到貓，是牠起身伸懶腰、走來聞籃子。[src: benjamin-bunny ¶38]
  - 原文："The cat got up and stretched herself, and came and sniffed at the basket." [src: benjamin-bunny ¶38]
- 貓聞了聞籃子，坐到籃子上面，一坐就是五個小時。[src: benjamin-bunny ¶38-41]
  - 原文："She sat there for five hours." [src: benjamin-bunny ¶41]
- 籃子底下很黑，洋蔥味很可怕，把兩隻兔子都熏哭了。[src: benjamin-bunny ¶42]
  - 原文："it was quite dark, and because the smell of onions was fearful" [src: benjamin-bunny ¶42]
- 原文只寫 "The cat"，沒有說是白貓，也沒有說是否就是第一篇池塘邊的那隻，不能推定為同一隻。[src: benjamin-bunny ¶38] [src: peter-rabbit ¶27]

### 6. 爸爸來救
- 牆上落下灰泥，貓抬頭看到 old Mr. Benjamin Bunny 在上層梯台的牆頂上蹦跳。[src: benjamin-bunny ¶44-45]
  - 原文："The cat looked up and saw old Mr. Benjamin Bunny prancing along the top of the wall of the upper terrace." [src: benjamin-bunny ¶45]
- 他抽著兔子菸草的菸斗、拿著一根細鞭子，正在找兒子。[src: benjamin-bunny ¶46-47]
  - 原文："He was looking for his son." [src: benjamin-bunny ¶47]
- 他一點也不把貓放在眼裡："Old Mr. Bunny had no opinion whatever of cats." [src: benjamin-bunny ¶48]
- 他從牆上跳到貓身上，把牠從籃子上打下來、踢進溫室並鎖上門。[src: benjamin-bunny ¶49-51]
  - 原文："When old Mr. Bunny had driven the cat into the greenhouse, he locked the door." [src: benjamin-bunny ¶51]
  - 原文："He took a tremendous jump off the top of the wall on to the top of the cat" [src: benjamin-bunny ¶49]
- 他拎著耳朵把 Benjamin 拉出來，用鞭子打了他，再拉出姪子/外甥（原文 nephew）Peter 和那包洋蔥，走出花園。[src: benjamin-bunny ¶52-54]
  - 原文："took out his son Benjamin by the ears, and whipped him with the little switch." [src: benjamin-bunny ¶52]
  - 原文："Then he took out his nephew Peter." [src: benjamin-bunny ¶53]

### 7. 結局
- Mr. McGregor 半小時後回來，看到很小的木鞋腳印，又不懂貓怎麼會被從外面鎖在溫室裡，覺得很困惑。[src: benjamin-bunny ¶55-57]
  - 原文："he observed several things which perplexed him." [src: benjamin-bunny ¶55]
  - 原文："only the footmarks were too ridiculously little!" [src: benjamin-bunny ¶56]
  - 原文："he could not understand how the cat could have managed to shut herself up inside the greenhouse, locking the door upon the outside." [src: benjamin-bunny ¶57]
- Peter 回家，媽媽因為他找回鞋子和外套就原諒了他；洋蔥和香草、兔子菸草一起掛在廚房天花板上。[src: benjamin-bunny ¶58]
  - 原文："When Peter got home his mother forgave him, because she was so glad to see that he had found his shoes and coat." [src: benjamin-bunny ¶58]

- 第一篇 Peter 說從表哥/表弟 Benjamin 那裡聽說過貓；本篇兩人真的一起被貓困住。[src: benjamin-bunny ¶37-41] [src: peter-rabbit ¶27]
