# 總結：彼得兔故事集是什麼？
- ① 一開始，調皮的 Peter 闖進花園 ｜ "But Peter, who was very naughty" [wiki: family-five-stories]
- ③ 後來，Benjamin 長大娶了 Flopsy，有了孩子 ｜ "When Benjamin Bunny grew up, he married his Cousin Flopsy." [wiki: family-five-stories]
- ④ 長大的 Peter 和 Benjamin 一起救回孩子，三代住在一起 ｜ "He lived with his son Benjamin Bunny and his daughter-in-law Flopsy" [wiki: family-five-stories]
- ⑤（推論）在 ③ 救過小兔子的林鼠，有了自己的故事 ｜ "Once upon a time there was a wood mouse, and her name was Mrs. Tittlemouse." [wiki: family-five-stories]
- 所以：前四篇是一個兔子家族長大的故事，第五篇靠一隻林鼠接上（推論） ｜ "she was called Thomasina Tittlemouse, a wood mouse with a long tail." [wiki: family-five-stories]
