# ③ The Tale of the Flopsy Bunnies：小兔子進了麻袋
- Benjamin 和 Flopsy 一家不太會存糧，但很快樂（improvident＝不懂得存糧） ｜ "they were very improvident and cheerful." [wiki: improvident]
- 孩子們跑到 McGregor 的垃圾堆，那裡什麼都有 ｜ "Mr. McGregor's rubbish heap was a mixture." [wiki: mr-mcgregors-rubbish-heap]
- Mr. McGregor 數到六隻，裝進麻袋（leetle 是 little 的口音拼法） ｜ "One, two, three, four! five! six leetle rabbits!" [wiki: flopsy-bunnies]
- 林鼠 Thomasina Tittlemouse（文中也叫 Mrs. Tittlemouse）咬破麻袋救了他們 ｜ "But Mrs. Tittlemouse was a resourceful person." [wiki: resourceful]
