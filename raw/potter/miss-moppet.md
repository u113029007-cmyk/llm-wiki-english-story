# The Story of Miss Moppet

¶1 This is a Pussy called Miss Moppet, she thinks she has heard a mouse!

¶2 This is the Mouse peeping out behind the cupboard, and making fun of Miss Moppet. He is not afraid of a kitten.

¶3 This is Miss Moppet jumping just too late; she misses the Mouse and hits her own head.

¶4 She thinks it is a very hard cupboard!

¶5 The Mouse watches Miss Moppet from the top of the cupboard.

¶6 Miss Moppet ties up her head in a duster, and sits before the fire.

¶7 The Mouse thinks she is looking very ill. He comes sliding down the bellpull.

¶8 Miss Moppet looks worse and worse. The Mouse comes a little nearer.

¶9 Miss Moppet holds her poor head in her paws, and looks at him through a hole in the duster. The Mouse comes very close.

¶10 And then all of a sudden﻿—Miss Moppet jumps upon the Mouse!

¶11 And because the Mouse has teased Miss Moppet﻿—Miss Moppet thinks she will tease the Mouse; which is not at all nice of Miss Moppet.

¶12 She ties him up in the duster, and tosses it about like a ball.

¶13 But she forgot about that hole in the duster; and when she untied it﻿—there was no Mouse!

¶14 He has wriggled out and run away; and he is dancing a jig on the top of the cupboard!
